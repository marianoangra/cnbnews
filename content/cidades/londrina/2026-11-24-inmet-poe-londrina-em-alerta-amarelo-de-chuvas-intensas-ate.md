The INMET has issued a yellow alert for Londrina, indicating possible heavy rainfall with up to 50 mm of precipitation and a high risk of flooding. This alert requires residents to be cautious, especially in low-lying areas prone to flooding.

Residents are advised to avoid areas at risk, such as riverbanks and low-lying regions, and to stay informed through official channels.
